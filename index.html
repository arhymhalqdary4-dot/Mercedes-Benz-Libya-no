Who we are

A long road, properly kept

Mercedes-Benz has been on Libyan roads for over half a century. Our job is to make sure the next fifty years are served as well as the last.

The first Mercedes-Benz saloons reached Tripoli in the 1960s. Many of them are still running — which is the point.

That reputation was not built on advertising. It was built on cars that kept working through heat, dust and distance, and on the people who kept them working. We inherited both halves of that promise.

As the authorised distributor, we hold the factory relationship directly: vehicles specified for this region, parts shipped against your chassis number, diagnostic software updated from Stuttgart, and technicians certified to the same standard as any centre in Europe.

The range is changing faster now than at any point in its history. Electric drivetrains, over-the-air updates, cabins that recognise who is sitting in them. Our commitment is that Libya gets that generation properly supported — not sold a car and left to find help.
